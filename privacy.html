Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 13, 2026

At MyClassHub, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our services to you:

  • Personal Identification Information: If you are a staff member, we process your email address for authentication purposes via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • User-Generated Content: Any content you input, including classroom announcements, homework assignments, schedule details, or support messages, is collected and stored within our database (Google Firestore).
  • Usage Data: We may collect technical information such as device identifiers and access logs to maintain the security and operational integrity of the system.

2. Use of Information

The information we collect is utilized exclusively for:

  • Managing user authentication and authorization levels.
  • Facilitating administrative tasks, such as posting homework and announcements.
  • Enabling secure communication between students and staff via our support system.
  • Maintaining the security and functionality of the MyClassHub platform.

3. Data Protection & Security

Your data is hosted via Google Firebase, which adheres to rigorous enterprise-grade security standards. While no method of digital transmission or storage is entirely impenetrable, we employ industry-standard protocols to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We utilize browser local storage solely to enhance your user experience by persisting your preferences, such as theme settings (Dark/Light mode) and active authentication sessions. We do not employ third-party tracking cookies or analytic trackers that monitor your browsing behavior across external websites.

5. Data Retention

We retain user-generated content and personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by institutional policy and applicable law. Users may contact the administrator for requests regarding data deletion.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our data protection practices.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the administrator via our internal support chat system.
