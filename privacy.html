Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 13, 2026

At MyClassHub, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our services to you:

  • Personal Identification Information: If you are a staff member, we process your email address for authentication purposes.
  • User-Generated Content: Any content you input, including classroom announcements, homework assignments, schedule details, or support messages, is collected and stored within our database.
  • Usage Data: We may collect technical information such as device identifiers and access logs to maintain the security and operational integrity of the system.

2. Use of Firebase Services

MyClassHub utilizes Google Firebase infrastructure to power our services:

  • Authentication: Google Firebase Authentication for secure user logins.
  • Database: Google Firestore for real-time data storage.
  • Hosting: Firebase Hosting for platform accessibility.

3. Use of Information

The information we collect is utilized exclusively for:

  • Managing user authentication and authorization levels.
  • Facilitating administrative tasks, such as posting homework and announcements.
  • Enabling secure communication between students and staff.
  • Maintaining the security and functionality of the platform.

4. Administrative Logging & Security

Administrative actions are logged for security, troubleshooting, and accountability purposes to ensure the integrity of the classroom environment.

5. Data Protection & Content Moderation

We employ industry-standard protocols to protect your personal data. Furthermore, we reserve the right to remove content that violates our policies, including spam, harassment, hate speech, threats, or inappropriate content.

6. Data Deletion

Users may request the deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting the administrator. Please note that certain records may be retained when required for school administration, security, or legal compliance.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

We utilize browser local storage solely to enhance your user experience by persisting your preferences, such as theme settings and authentication sessions. We do not employ third-party tracking cookies.

8. Age Restrictions

MyClassHub is intended for use by students, teachers, teaching assistants, and school staff. Users under the age of 18 should use the platform under the supervision of their educational institution.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, please contact the administrator via our internal support chat system.
